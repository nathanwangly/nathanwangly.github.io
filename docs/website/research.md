# Research

This is guidance for site work, not approved research copy or publication claims.

## Current implementation

- `research.md` introduces Nathan's research and renders journal articles and other publications from `_data/publications.yml`.
- Entries are sorted by year, newest first, within each category. They show bibliographic details and available Web or PDF links. Styling lives in `assets/css/research.css`.
- The page uses the shared `page` layout and site navigation.

## Confirmed direction

- Research background remains one of the site's main areas. It should feel part of the same site and work well on mobile and desktop.

## Open choices

- No research-specific redesign or different publication grouping has been settled.
