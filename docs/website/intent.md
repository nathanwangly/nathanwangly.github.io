# Website intent

## Purpose

The site offers an understated introduction to Nathan and his work, and provides a home for projects or writing he chooses to share. It should be there for people who encounter his name or a link to the site, without making self-promotion its focus.

## Audience

The audience is intentionally broad. It may include peers or prospective employers who arrive via LinkedIn and want a fuller sense of Nathan’s background and interests than a résumé alone provides.

## Priorities

Balance a light, polished presentation with a low-key presence. The site should convey personality through its design and the tone of its content. Nathan writes the site copy himself.

## Signs of success

The site feels like a considered expression of Nathan’s character: minimalist, thoughtful, low-key, with a little bit of fun. It also gives him a natural place to share projects or posts when he chooses.
